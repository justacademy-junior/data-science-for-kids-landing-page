Four steps to any data story

Whether it is a lemonade stand or a space mission, a good data story follows the same path.

1

Ask

Pick one clear question, like which pet is the class favourite.

2

Collect

Count, measure and tidy up your answers so they match.

3

Study

Find the biggest, the smallest and the average. Spot the pattern.

4

Tell

Draw it, say it and share it in words anyone can follow.
